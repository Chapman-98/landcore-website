OUR STORY

How It All Started

Founded in the early 1990s, Landcore began with a simple vision: to deliver exceptional construction projects through quality craftsmanship, professionalism, and genuine care for our clients and community.

From our humble beginnings to becoming a trusted name across West Virginia, Ohio, and North Carolina, we've never lost sight of what matters most—the people behind every project and the communities we serve.

Today, with 33+ years of experience and over 1,500 units developed, we continue to stay ahead of the curve by incorporating cutting-edge technology like GPS grading systems and investing in the personal development of our team members.

What Drives Us

"We don't just move dirt; we build the foundation for West Virginia's future."

Every road we pave, every building we construct, and every site we develop becomes part of the infrastructure that connects families, businesses, and communities. Our work touches the daily lives of thousands—from the morning commute to school, to the businesses that drive our local economy.

We take pride in knowing that our projects will serve generations to come, and we approach each one with unwavering attention to detail, creativity, and innovation.

What We Do

General Contracting

Our extensive experience allows us to offer comprehensive solutions including utility and zoning requirements, design, site development, and building construction. We take a detail-oriented approach to construction, ensuring that every aspect of the project is executed with the highest level of quality and safety.

Custom Metal Buildings

Proud Authorized Builder for Nucor Building Systems with over 25 years of expertise in custom engineered metal building systems. We self-perform approximately 60% of all our Nucor building projects, allowing us to control quality and deliver exceptional results with energy-efficient, sustainable solutions.

Comprehensive Development Services

From land acquisition to build-ready status, we provide a seamless process that includes site selection, land acquisition, planning, and preparing land for construction. Our end-to-end project management ensures timely completion and community integration.
