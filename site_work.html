Site Work & Infrastructure

Landcore has built a reputation as one of West Virginia's most reliable site work contractors, delivering projects that form the foundation of commercial, residential, and municipal developments across the region. Our experienced crews bring decades of combined expertise to every job, from small residential grading to large-scale commercial site preparation.

We take pride in our ability to handle complex site conditions and challenging terrain—something that's essential in the Mountain State. By maintaining our own fleet of modern equipment and employing skilled operators year-round, we're able to respond quickly to project needs and maintain the quality standards our clients expect.

Why Landcore

Experienced Crews

Our site work teams include operators and supervisors with decades of experience in West Virginia's unique terrain and soil conditions.

Modern Equipment

We maintain a fleet of late-model excavators, dozers, and grading equipment to ensure efficiency and precision on every project.

Local Knowledge

33+ years of operating in the Tri-State area means we understand local regulations, soil conditions, and what it takes to get permits approved.

Full-Service Capability

From initial clearing to final grade, we self-perform the majority of site work, giving you a single point of accountability.

Our Site Work Services

Grading & Earthwork

Precision grading for building pads, parking lots, and roadways using GPS-guided equipment.

Erosion Control

Silt fencing, sediment basins, and erosion control measures that meet DEP requirements.

Storm Drainage

Complete storm water management including culverts, retention ponds, and drainage systems.

Utility Installation

Water, sewer, gas, and electric conduit installation for residential and commercial sites.

Paving & Asphalt

Asphalt paving for driveways, parking lots, and access roads.

Concrete Work

Curbs, sidewalks, foundations, and flatwork for commercial and residential projects.

Demolition

Safe demolition and removal of existing structures to prepare sites for new construction.

Land Clearing

Tree removal, brush clearing, and grubbing to prepare raw land for development.
