Land Development

With over 33 years of experience and more than 1,500 units developed across West Virginia, Ohio, and North Carolina, Landcore has established itself as a trusted partner for residential and commercial land development projects of all sizes.

We handle the entire development process—from raw land acquisition and due diligence through final infrastructure and build-ready lots. Our track record speaks for itself: zero unfinished projects and long-standing relationships with builders, investors, and municipalities throughout the Tri-State region.
