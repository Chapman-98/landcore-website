Civil Foreman / Superintendent

Full-Time Milton, WV
Apply Now

We are seeking a highly skilled and experienced Civil Site Foreman/Superintendent to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a background in road construction, utilities, commercial building sites, and developments.

Responsibilities

  • Plan and manage daily operations on construction sites
  • Review daily and weekly production goals with project managers
  • Prepare daily logs to track progress and ensure efficient operations
  • Coordinate with subcontractors to ensure work is completed on schedule
  • Communicate effectively with inspectors, vendors, and project managers
  • Ensure quality control and company safety policies are followed

Skills & Qualifications

  • Minimum 5 years' experience in heavy civil construction
  • Heavy equipment operation experience preferred
  • Excellent project management and supervision skills
  • Strong decision-making ability and leadership skills
  • Proficiency in Microsoft Office
  • Some travel may be required

Surveyor / Layout Professional

Full-Time Milton, WV
Apply Now

We are seeking a highly skilled and motivated Surveyor/Layout professional to join our team. The ideal candidate will have a passion for the construction industry and a desire to contribute to the success of our projects.

Responsibilities

  • Create CAD models for field layout and machine control using CAD and Trimble Business Center
  • Perform construction layout using GPS and Total Station technology
  • Assist estimators with model creation and material takeoffs
  • Manage GPS technology and other layout equipment
  • Review daily project reports and provide technical support
  • Travel between projects and the office as needed

Skills & Qualifications

  • Bachelor's degree in Civil Engineering, Land Surveying, or Construction Management
  • Experience with CAD or Trimble Business Center
  • Advanced MS Office skills
  • Excellent project management and organizational skills
  • Strong decision-making ability and leadership skills
  • Familiarity with construction industry standards and regulations
